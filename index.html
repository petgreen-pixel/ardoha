    About Ardoha

    A book-focused marketing agency helping authors around the world reach readers, build influence and fulfil their publishing dreams.

    Our story

    Ardoha began with a simple belief: a book that deserves readers should never have to struggle to find them.

    Every year, countless authors pour months, often years, into a manuscript. They edit, perfect the cover, press publish, and then watch their book disappear into one of the most crowded marketplaces in the world. The writing is good. The story matters. What’s missing isn’t talent. It’s visibility, and a clear plan for turning that visibility into readers.

    Ardoha was built to close that gap. We’re a digital marketing agency that works with books, and that focus shapes everything we do. We understand how readers discover, doubt and decide. We know how Amazon, Goodreads, Google Play, YouTube and email each work for authors. And we bring strategy, creativity and honest reporting together, so you always know what’s happening and why.

    Today we work with authors around the world, from first-time self-published writers preparing a debut to established names ready for brand partnerships and screen opportunities. The stage may differ, but the approach stays the same: understand the reader, polish every touchpoint, and tell you plainly what’s working.

    Beyond visibility. Into influence. Being seen is only the beginning. We want your book, and your name as an author, to be remembered and trusted.
